# Product Vision

## Product

A personal learning application that teaches mathematics and foundational scientific reasoning from the ground up.

The app identifies missing prerequisites, teaches concepts in dependency order, provides guided and independent practice, diagnoses mistakes, schedules review, and verifies long-term mastery.

## Problem

Many learners have uneven knowledge. They may understand some advanced ideas while missing basic foundations. Traditional courses usually follow a fixed sequence and do not reliably identify the exact prerequisite causing difficulty.

General-purpose AI tutors can explain concepts, but they do not consistently maintain a verified curriculum, deterministic grading system, mastery record, or long-term review schedule.

## Intended User

The initial user is an adult learner who may:

- Have forgotten school mathematics
- Have inconsistent foundations
- Want plain-language explanations
- Need unlimited repetition
- Prefer to understand why a method works
- Want measurable progression rather than disconnected answers

## Product Promise

At any point, the app should be able to determine:

- What the learner understands
- What the learner has forgotten
- Which prerequisite is missing
- What should be studied next
- Why an answer was wrong
- Whether the learner is ready to advance

## Educational Principles

1. Foundations before advanced material
2. Understanding before memorization
3. Intuition before formal notation
4. Active practice before passive review
5. Specific error diagnosis instead of generic correction
6. Mastery based on retention, not only immediate accuracy
7. Mixed practice to develop method selection
8. Deterministic grading wherever possible
9. AI assistance without AI control over verified truth
10. Progress at the learner's pace

## Initial Subject Scope

### Mathematics

Basic arithmetic through introductory algebra.

### Science Foundation

Scientific reasoning, measurement, units, estimation, experimental thinking, tables, and graphs.

## Long-Term Direction

The same learning engine should later support:

- Geometry
- Algebra II
- Statistics
- Probability
- Trigonometry
- Calculus
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Biology
- Astronomy
- Computer science
- Logic
- Economics
- Other structured subjects

## Success Definition

The first version succeeds when a learner with weak or uneven mathematics foundations can begin at an appropriate point, repair prerequisite gaps, progress through introductory algebra, retain learned material, and understand why errors occurred.
